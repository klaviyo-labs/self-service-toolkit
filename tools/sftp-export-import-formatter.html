Klaviyo SFTP Reimport

Converts a Klaviyo SFTP export CSV into an import-ready CSV. Emails are matched by profile_id from the segment CSV you provide. Rows with no email match are saved to a separate errors CSV.

  1. Use SFTP Export to export the desired metric's events from your Klaviyo account.
  2. Export a segment CSV capturing every profile that has performed this metric over all time.
  3. Enter the Metric Name below, then upload both files to their respective drop zones.
  4. Click Download CSV to get the reformatted file. If any rows were skipped, click Download Errors CSV to review them.
  5. Upload the reformatted CSV to the Klaviyo SFTP server under Import → Events.
Applied to every row in the output as the "Metric Name" column.
